### What I believe about AI use when writing
My personal policy for when writing is that at AI shouldn't be used for writing the actual words in the finished piece. I am at least grudgingly accepting of most other uses for AI during the writing process, as long as a human at least double checks any sources to at least make sure that they exist.
My main reasons for this are mostly just that if I am reading something, I want the actual words to be written by a human. Even if the entire premise and research, and everything but the actual act of putting words on the page is done entirely by an AI. I at least want for the words I am reading to have been written by a fellow human. This is one reason why I support AI text watermarking, as said watermarking makes it easier to check if AI was used in the final writing/editing of the piece.
