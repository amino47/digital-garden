Many people are misinformed on how Claude text watermarking is going to work.

Anthropic has announced that it will start to watermark AI text generated by Claude models starting on August 2nd 2026. This is noteworthy because of the major community backlash that this announcement sparked, and how much of this backlash is misinformed.
One major aspect of the watermarking that many people seem to not understand is that the way the text is being watermarked will have no negative impact on the quality of generated responses. Furthermore, many people fundamentally misunderstand the mechanisms by which AIs generate text, and how the watermarking affects that. AI models use a mix of [[Pseudo-Random Number Generation]] and a weighted probability based on training data to predict the next word. The proposed Claude watermarks work by making the seed a known value, which lets the checkers simply check if the text might have been generated with the known watermark seed, outputting a confidence score.
